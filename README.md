# Let’s Live with Popo — onboarding demo

A public family-review draft of all six chapters, Indonesian by default with English selectable. Main guidance is visible by scrolling. Each Listen button reads only its own card, including that card’s quotation, message template or photo captions, using the device/browser voice and stops at the end. No autoplay or whole-chapter narration.

## Chapters

1. Who Popo is — her life, pride, preferences and family context.
2. What dementia is doing to her — understanding feelings and everyday changes.
3. Making a good day — six familiar day moments, with separate outings, activities, food and comfort guidance.
4. When days get hard — the original three situations and related ones, joint family support and emergency boundaries.
5. We carry this together — the helper’s rest, sleep, feelings, personal life, support and gradual handover.
6. Practical matters — visible WhatsApp formats, BP records, current medicine-plan process, personal care, safety, household tasks, shopping, appointments and the shared cookbook.

The confirmed-food revision changes only Chapter 6’s cookbook/menu ideas and related photographs/captions. Chapters 1–5 and all non-food Chapter 6 guidance are preserved. The Chapter 3 timeline still has six moments and four genuine Indonesian helper excerpts; original spelling is preserved and English is explicitly labelled translation. Authored guidance and suggested dialogue are not presented as real quotations.

The cookbook has 44 source-backed food/reference cards, the nine newly identified original pictures, the newly supplied dinner and sweet-soup pictures, and clearly labelled tofu reference illustrations. It is organised by breakfast, snacks, meals, drinks and recipe ideas, with seven flexible menu ideas rather than a compulsory schedule. Wonton preparation and the finished result are visible together, with no carousel or extra required click. Begin with favourites and learn through shopping/cooking together. Unknown quantities, cooking times, ingredients and medical benefits are not guessed from pictures. Existing recipe/video links remain optional references, not represented as fully ingredient-checked.

Specific family confirmations include fish-filled aubergine and tofu pockets without beef for that dish; plain steamed tofu once every two weeks, not an egg restriction; Shanghai chicken wontons made with Popo’s remembered wrapping skills and optional cooking wine; weekend painting-break dragon fruit; yellow-sugar sweet-potato/snow-fungus sweet soup; separate egg pancake and tomato/egg dishes; eel and chicken-feet pot rice; dried-lily-bud/cloud-ear steamed chicken; fish/choy-sum; and curry-block chicken curry. Soup-specific instructions stay specific to that soup.

## Visibility and care boundaries

This GitHub Pages demo is public and **not password protected**. Noindex is an indexing instruction, not access control. Text and photographs can be downloaded. Final family review and confirmed-new-hire readiness remain separate from this requested publication.

Private household contact information must never be published, even behind a password. Contact and location arrangements are supplied separately. No private number, email, address, contact list, raw feedback/source record, identity document or identifying prescription image is included. Public emergency 999 is allowed.

Verified medication instructions may be published with the owner’s permission, but the current transcription is incomplete and has schedule conflicts. The medicine-reference fields remain visibly **PENDING**, not an administration chart or a new regimen. Use the current written doctor/pharmacist plan; do not invent a dose, timing, escalation, repeat, crushing or splitting rule. This food revision does not change medication instructions.

Personal names are omitted from current reader copy, narration, image captions/index and helper asset paths; Popo remains the established nickname. Historical Git commits/tags and earlier downloaded copies are not rewritten or erased by a forward update. The photographs are real supplied photographs, not generated lookalikes. Three library copies obscure incidental background writing for privacy; user originals remain unchanged and private.

No backend, database, WhatsApp integration, generated narration, authentication or source tools are deployed. The applicant website is a separate repository and is not modified here.

## Hosting and versions

GitHub Pages serves `main` at the repository root. No build step; `.nojekyll` disables Jekyll processing. Asset/module/data paths are relative to the project URL. Updates use ordinary forward commits and a pre-update rollback tag; never force-push or rewrite history. A requested rollback restores a prior version through a new forward commit.

Indonesian voice availability depends on the phone/browser and may require installing a matching voice. A missing voice is explained rather than replaced with English. Actual sound still needs a phone check.
