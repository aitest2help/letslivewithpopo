Open the [illustrated bilingual index](INDEX.md) or the [Copilot-readable manifest](manifest.json). These files are publicly accessible.

The nine formerly unidentified food pictures now have family-confirmed captions. Real family photographs and the two externally sourced tofu reference illustrations are separate categories; an illustration does not establish the family's recipe or the pictured filling. Do not describe a reference as the helper's own cooking. The first aubergine reference bearing a personal-name watermark is not republished; its watermark has not been erased.

User originals remain private and unchanged. Library copies omit embedded metadata, and three copies have incidental background writing hidden for privacy. Those edits are noted in the manifest/index; people, dishes and full framing are retained. The manifest preserves previous source aliases to support authoring without copying private research or contact documents.

Chapter 6 presents meal-time ideas, not a compulsory daily menu. Keep confirmed ingredients, sides, occasions and dish-specific frequency faithful to the family notes. No ingredient, quantity, cooking time or medical benefit should be inferred from appearance. Names other than Popo and private contacts must stay off the public page, captions, alt text and narration.
